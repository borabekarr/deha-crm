Voice Beam

voice-beam source: prose

A sound-reactive glow along the bottom edge of a wrapped child element: a centered, colorful beam that rises and blooms with the voice. Variants: default (chat input), pill (recording), mobile (phone-bottom edge). States: idle, live, processing, disabled. Voice level below is simulated (a fake 0-1 envelope), not a real microphone.
